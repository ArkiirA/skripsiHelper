# One-time payment per project, daily AI cap for free tier

Monetization uses a one-time payment per skripsi project to unlock unlimited AI features. Free tier gets 5 AI actions per day.

**Why one-time over subscription:** Indonesian students are price-sensitive and distrust recurring charges. A single payment ("Rp 50.000 untuk buka fitur AI skripsimu") is a lower psychological barrier than monthly billing. Students have a defined end date (graduation) — subscriptions feel wasteful after sidang.

**Why daily cap over monthly:** Skripsi deadlines create intense bursts of activity. A monthly cap punishes students exactly when they need the tool most. Daily cap resets hope every morning and smooths usage.

**Considered alternatives:**
- Monthly subscription: rejected for student price sensitivity and distrust of recurring billing
- Credit packs: rejected for added complexity in an already stressful workflow
- University partnerships: not rejected but deferred — possible future channel, not the launch model
