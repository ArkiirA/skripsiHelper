# Warm approachable design with teal accent and Plus Jakarta Sans

Visual design follows a "warm & approachable" direction: neutral base (white/warm gray), single teal accent color, Plus Jakarta Sans typography, system dark mode support.

**Why warm over minimal/bold/academic:** Students use this tool under deadline stress. Warm and approachable lowers anxiety — Canva proved this approach works for intimidating creative tasks in the Indonesian market. Notion-style minimalism feels cold for stressed students. Duolingo energy is wrong for thesis writing. Academic classic reminds them of poorly-designed university portals (SIAKAD).

**Why Plus Jakarta Sans:** Designed by Indonesian typographer Gumpita Rahayu. Geometric, highly legible, free on Google Fonts, good weight range for UI hierarchy. Subtle cultural nod without gimmick.

**Why teal accent:** Reads as calm-but-trustworthy. Works in both light and dark themes. Single accent keeps the writing interface distraction-free — students stare at this for hours.

**Dark mode:** Respects `prefers-color-scheme`, ships light as default. Non-negotiable for a writing tool students use at 2 AM. Implemented via CSS custom properties.
