# AI assists but never generates skripsi content

AI features (Gemini) are used for suggestion, summarization, paraphrasing, and paper discovery — never for generating thesis content wholesale.

**Why:** Indonesian universities treat AI-generated content as plagiarism. The tool's credibility depends on being a writing aid, not a ghostwriter. Students must be able to defend every sentence in their sidang (thesis defense). AI that suggests structure and helps comprehend source material is safe; AI that writes paragraphs is a liability for the student and the product.

**Boundaries:** Outline suggestion from topic + pedoman, paraphrasing selected text, summarizing uploaded papers, academic paper search query reformulation, grammar/style checking for Bahasa Indonesia academic writing, citation format validation. Content generation (drafting paragraphs, writing sections) is explicitly excluded.
